# Risk-Reward Loop

## Overview

This is the engine behind "one more turn" syndrome. Every meaningful choice presents a tradeoff: push forward for potential gain, or play safe and miss out. The best risk-reward loops make you WANT to take the risk, even when the odds are bad.

## Core loop

1. Player faces a choice with uncertain outcome
2. Potential reward is visible/attractive
3. Potential risk is clear but tempting
4. Player commits to a choice
5. Outcome resolves — payoff or punishment
6. Player learns and adjusts strategy for next decision

## Risk archetypes

### Resource risk
- **Health**: Spend HP to deal more damage (Hades bloodless builds)
- **Currency**: Bet money you might lose (roguelike shops)
- **Items**: Use a rare consumable now vs. save it
- **Ammo**: Fire the good weapon or save it for later

### Position risk
- **Aggressive positioning**: Get close for more damage but expose yourself
- **Exploration**: Go off the main path for secrets but risk getting lost
- **Stealth vs. loud**: Stealth is safer but slower
- **Height**: High ground advantage but fall damage risk

### Time risk
- **Rush**: Complete faster for bonus but miss preparation
- **Wait**: Prepare thoroughly but miss time-sensitive rewards
- **Side quests**: Do them for XP but risk main mission difficulty

### Information risk
- **Scouting**: Spend resources to reveal fog of war
- **Commitment**: Lock into a build/class without knowing what's ahead
- **Trust**: Help an NPC that might betray you

## Design dimensions

### Reward scaling
- **Linear**: Same reward regardless of risk level
- **Exponential**: Higher risk = disproportionately higher reward
- **Threshold**: Certain risk levels unlock bonus tiers
- **Random**: Risk doesn't guarantee reward (gambling feel)

### Failure consequences
- **Mild** (Hades): Lose gold, keep meta-progression
- **Moderate** (XCOM): Lose squad members permanently
- **Severe** (roguelikes): Lose everything, start over
- **None** (some games): Free attempts, no real cost

### Player agency
- **Full control** (Civilization): Player chooses when to take risks
- **Forced** (some events): No safe option, must choose risk
- **Opt-in** (Hades): Can play safe or push for bonus rewards

### Information availability
- **Full information**: Know exact odds before choosing
- **Partial information**: Some clues, but uncertain
- **Hidden**: Can't know the outcome until committed

## Anti-patterns

- **No risk**: If there's no downside, there's no decision
- **No reward**: If the safe option is always better, why risk?
- **False risk**: If the "risky" option is actually safe, the loop breaks
- **Punishment without learning**: If failure feels random, players stop trying

## References

- XCOM 2: Every mission is a gamble with permadeath on the line
- Hades: Boon choices and heat levels as risk-reward levers
- Civilization VI: "One more turn" is literally the risk-reward loop
- Monster Hunter Rise: Aggressive playstyles rewarded but punished if you fail
