# Enemy Variety

## Overview

A game with one enemy type is a game with one strategy. Enemy variety forces players to adapt, switch tactics, and think on their feet. The best enemy rosters create emergent complexity — two easy enemies together become a hard encounter. Doom Eternal makes you switch weapons for each demon type. That's variety done right.

## Core loop

1. Player encounters new enemy type
2. Player observes enemy behavior and weaknesses
3. Player adapts strategy to counter the enemy
4. Multiple enemy types appear together
5. Player must manage multiple threats simultaneously

## Enemy archetypes

### Frontline (Hollow Knight bugs)
- **Direct combat**: Charges at player
- **Simple pattern**: Easy to read, hard to avoid in groups
- **Basic counter**: Hit them before they hit you
- **Group pressure**: Dangerous in numbers

### Ranged (Doom Eternal Imps)
- **Projectile attacks**: Forces player to dodge or close distance
- **Positioning**: Stays at distance, annoying to reach
- **Disruption**: Interrupts player's other actions
- **Counter**: Close distance or use ranged attacks

### Tank (Doom Eternal Mancubus)
- **High health**: Takes many hits to defeat
- **Heavy attacks**: Slow but devastating
- **Area denial**: Controls space with attacks
- **Counter**: Flank, heavy weapons, or avoidance

### Support (Hollow Knight Hovering Knights)
- **Buff/heal other enemies**: Must be prioritized
- **Low health**:脆弱 but impactful
- **Disruption**: Creates problems for the player
- **Counter**: Kill first, deal with others after

### Ambush (Dark Souls mimics)
- **Hidden until triggered**: Surprise attacks
- **High damage**: Punishes unprepared players
- **Environmental**: Uses terrain to advantage
- **Counter**: Awareness, preparation, caution

### Elite (Monster Hunter apex predators)
- **Enhanced versions**: Stronger than standard enemies
- **Unique mechanics**: Special attacks or behaviors
- **Reward**: Better loot for defeating
- **Counter**: Mastery of basic enemy mechanics

## Design dimensions

### Combat role
- **Damage dealer**: High DPS, priority target
- **Controller**: Area denial, slows player
- **Support**: Buffs allies, heals, resurrects
- **Tank**: Absorbs damage, protects allies
- **Disruptor**: Interrupts player actions, causes chaos

### Counter-play clarity
- **Obvious weakness**: Visual tells show how to defeat
- **Learnable**: Pattern recognition rewards experience
- **Multiple counters**: Several strategies work
- **No hard counter**: Skill beats any approach

### Encounter composition
- **Single type**: One enemy type per encounter
- **Mixed types**: Multiple enemies creating complexity
- **Escalation**: Start simple, add variety gradually
- **Boss integration**: Regular enemies appear with bosses

### Learning curve
- **Introduction**: New enemy introduced solo
- **Practice**: Encounter gives time to learn
- **Combination**: New enemy mixed with known ones
- **Mastery**: Complex encounters with multiple types

## Anti-patterns

- **Reskinned enemies**: Same enemy with different colors = no variety
- **No counter-play**: If enemies can't be countered, they're just damage sponges
- **Overwhelming**: Too many enemy types at once = confusion
- **Underwhelming**: If all enemies feel the same to fight, variety is meaningless
- **No escalation**: If difficulty doesn't increase with variety, it's wasted
